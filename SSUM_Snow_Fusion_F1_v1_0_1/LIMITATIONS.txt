LIMITATIONS

SCOPE
- The reserved-period evaluation covers January-March 2026 at eight U.S. stations in the Northeast and Upper Midwest.
- A single reserved winter block cannot establish broad climatological generalization.
- Results may differ for other NBM versions, seasons, locations, snow regimes and event thresholds.

UNCERTAINTY
- The station-cluster bootstrap contains only eight clusters.
- The temporal bootstrap lower bound is positive but close to zero (+0.002057587939).
- The observed effect should be interpreted as modest rather than large.

DISCRIMINATION ONLY
- Fusion-F1 is a ranking score and is not asserted to be a calibrated probability.
- The study does not establish Brier-score superiority, reliability calibration, decision-curve superiority, cost-loss value or operational threshold benefit.

COMPARATOR
- The comparator is the declared archived NBM NBS joint score derived from P06 and PSN.
- The result does not establish superiority over every NBM snow field, model system or post-processing method.

SOURCE SEMANTICS
- The 2025 SPF1 reconstruction must preserve the historical feature convention used by the frozen predictor, including division of the raw GHCN SNOW integer by 10 for model-input construction.
- This historical model-input convention is not a statement that the source archive's native SNOW unit is tenths of millimetres.
- The 2025 truth path and 2026 truth path use raw GHCN SNOW only for the binary event test.
- The 2026 predictor path uses date-bounded NCEI Daily Summaries metric values exactly as the frozen R4 implementation did.

REPRODUCIBILITY
- Static package verification and self-tests are deterministic.
- Full source reconstruction requires access to the declared NOAA/NCEI sources.
- Source archives may change availability, transport behavior or metadata without changing the frozen scientific evidence recorded here.
- Project-source reconstruction is distinct from an independently authored replication.
