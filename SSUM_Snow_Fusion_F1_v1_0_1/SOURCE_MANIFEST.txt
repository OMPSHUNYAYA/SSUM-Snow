SOURCE MANIFEST

NOAA / NWS / NCEP NATIONAL BLEND OF MODELS

Archive root:
https://noaa-nbm-grib2-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/

File pattern:
https://noaa-nbm-grib2-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/blend.YYYYMMDD/01/text/blend_nbstx.t01z

Cycle:
01 UTC on target date D.

Fields:
S06 - six-hour forecast snow amount field used by the 2025 R3 case-admission rule.
P06 - six-hour probability of precipitation, percent.
PSN - conditional probability of snow, percent.

2025 R3:
A station-day is score-complete only when required local-day S06, P06 and PSN values are all present. NOAA -99 is missing.

2026 R4:
NBM_JOINT_DAY = max((P06/100)*(PSN/100)) over required six-hour endpoints on target-station local date D. S06 is not part of the R4 joint-only predictor parser.

NOAA / NCEI GHCN-DAILY RAW .DLY

Archive root:
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/all/

Format reference:
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/readme.txt

Elements used:
TMAX, TMIN, PRCP, SNOW.

Native raw units described by GHCN-Daily:
TMAX, TMIN - tenths of degrees C.
PRCP - tenths of millimetres.
SNOW - millimetres.

Quality handling:
-9999 is missing.
Nonblank QFLAG is inadmissible.

Historical 2025 SPF1 feature convention:
The frozen R3 predictor divided the raw integer by 10 for TMAX, TMIN, PRCP and SNOW before constructing SPF1 features. This convention is preserved solely to reconstruct the frozen model input.

Truth convention:
Raw SNOW integer > 0 is an event after the quality gate.

NOAA / NCEI DAILY SUMMARIES SERVICE

Endpoint:
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1

2026 R4 predictor request:
dataset=daily-summaries
dataTypes=TMAX,TMIN,PRCP,SNOW
units=metric
includeAttributes=false
format=json
startDate=D-2
endDate=D-1
stations=the 15 declared GHCN station identifiers

The numeric values returned by this service are consumed directly by the frozen R4 feature builder.
